PAWLINK / A CLOSER LOOK02 — AN ILLUSTRATIVE DAY
WHAT WOULD YOU NOTICE?

A quiet day.
Or a change?

Imagine a dog who usually loves a long afternoon walk. Today, they move less than usual.

A single reading tells only part of the story. Their own routine gives it context.

01 / What PawLink could show

Today’s activity alongside their usual pattern, with the day’s other signals available to explore. A significant departure from that pattern could surface as an alert.

02 / What you can do

Check in with your dog and consider the day: a different walk, a warm afternoon, more time resting. PawLink supports your attention; it does not explain the cause or make a diagnosis.
